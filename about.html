Our History

TechCon began in 2010 as a small gathering of technology enthusiasts and has since grown into one of the premier technology conferences in the world. Over the years, TechCon has hosted thousands of attendees, featured groundbreaking innovations, and fostered a vibrant community of tech professionals.

Our Mission

TechCon's mission is to inspire, educate, and connect the global technology community. We strive to provide a platform for sharing knowledge, exploring new ideas, and driving innovation in the tech industry.

Notable Past Speakers

Ada Lovelace

Renowned as the first computer programmer, Ada Lovelace inspired attendees with her vision of computing's future and its impact on society.

Grace Hopper

A pioneer in computer science, Grace Hopper's keynote on programming languages and innovation remains a highlight in TechCon history.

Tim Berners-Lee

Inventor of the World Wide Web, Tim Berners-Lee shared insights on the evolution of the internet and the importance of open access.
